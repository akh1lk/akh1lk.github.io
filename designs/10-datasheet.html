The AKH-1K is a Philly-born CS student at Cornell who likes building things that ship, from GPU kernels and tokenizer-free LLMs to agentic systems and full-stack apps. Primary interests are machine learning, systems, and quantitative finance.

Typical deployments span four layers of the stack simultaneously: a hand-written Triton kernel underneath, a replicated research architecture above it, an agentic retrieval layer around that, and a React interface that non-engineers actually open.
